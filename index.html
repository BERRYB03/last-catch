    Bait Master’s tip

    The running sinker: ball sinker (1–3 oz) running on the main line → swivel → 0.6–1 m of 20–25 lb trace → 4/0–5/0 recurve hook. Bait: half a pilchard (with bait elastic) or a squid strip. Lightest sinker that holds bottom, and keep the berley going. The best bait in the harbour is the first fresh kahawai or mullet you catch.

    Other rigs
    • Ledger / flasher: wharves; trevally, small snapper, kahawai. 2-hook dropper, 2/0–3/0 hooks, bomb sinker. Small baits, keeps them off the rays.
    • Stray line: just a 4/0–6/0 hook and bait, drifted back into the berley on the shallow flats on the flood. Deadly on neaps.
    • Soft baits: 3–5″ plastics on 1/8–1/4 oz jig heads for kayak drifting; up to 3/8 oz in the channel.
    • Metals / sabiki: 10–30 g slices for kahawai busting the surface; sabiki + a little berley for piper, mackerel and mullet.

    What’s biting (mid-Oct)

    KahawaiMost likely fight. Bleed straight away.
